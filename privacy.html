Hermes privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Who uses Hermes

Hermes is a private AI assistant operated by the owner of this site for their own use. It is not offered to anyone else, and it only accesses the Google account that its owner has explicitly authorised.

What Google data Hermes accesses

  • Gmail: reading and searching messages, sending and replying to messages, and changing labels.
  • Google Calendar: reading events and creating, changing or deleting events.

Hermes uses this data only to carry out requests its owner makes. Sending email and changing calendar events require the owner's explicit confirmation each time.

Where data is stored

Access tokens and conversation history are stored on the owner's private server. Backups are stored in the owner's personal Google Drive.

Sharing

To answer a request, the relevant content is sent to the AI model provider that powers Hermes (currently GitHub Copilot). Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with any other third party.

Information received from Google APIs is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
